
# Global Electric Vehicle Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of global Electric Vehicle (EV) adoption trends using Power BI. The dashboard explores regional growth patterns, vehicle category performance, powertrain adoption, energy consumption, sales trends, and year-over-year EV market expansion.

The objective is to transform raw EV data into actionable insights through interactive visualizations and data storytelling.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains global EV statistics with the following attributes:

* **Region** – Geographic region or country  
* **Category** – Vehicle type (Cars, Buses, Trucks, 2-Wheelers, etc.)  
* **Parameter** – Metric being measured (Stock, Sales, Energy Use, etc.)  
* **Mode** – Transport mode  
* **Powertrain** – BEV, PHEV, FCEV, and other EV technologies  
* **Year** – Reporting year  
* **Unit** – Measurement unit  
* **Value** – Numerical value of the metric  

---

## Business Objectives

### Regional & Category Analysis

* Analyze EV growth across regions over time.  
* Identify top contributing regions in EV adoption.  
* Compare EV penetration across vehicle categories.  
* Evaluate regional dominance in specific vehicle segments.  

### Powertrain Analysis

* Examine distribution of EV powertrains.  
* Identify fastest-growing powertrain technologies.  
* Compare powertrain adoption across transportation modes.  
* Analyze changes in powertrain trends from 2020 to 2024.  

### Parameter Analysis

* Evaluate stock, sales, and energy consumption metrics.  
* Identify regions contributing most to each parameter.  
* Compare stock growth and new registrations.  
* Analyze unit distribution across metrics.  

### Time-Series Analysis

* Study year-wise global EV growth.  
* Compare trends across leading regions. 
* Identify peak years for sales and stock.  
* Detect significant category-level growth spikes.

## Key Insights

* EV adoption has increased significantly across most regions over the years.  
* Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) dominate global EV deployment.  
* Certain regions contribute a major share of global EV sales and stock.  
* Passenger road transport represents the largest EV adoption segment.  
* EV energy consumption has grown alongside vehicle adoption.  
* Strong growth is observed in recent years due to increased electrification initiatives.
